# Software services agreement — Quillon Analytics

Customer: Halvorsen Design Studio
Vendor: Quillon Analytics Ltd.
Effective date: 2025-02-01
Contract reference: QA-HDS-0425

## Scope

Quillon provides a hosted web-analytics dashboard, monthly traffic
reports and up to ten hours of analyst support per quarter.

## Fees

Annual subscription fee of $18,000, invoiced quarterly in advance
($4,500 per quarter). Fees may rise by at most 4% at each renewal.

## Term and termination

The agreement renews automatically every twelve months. Either party may
terminate by giving 60 days' written notice before the renewal date.
On termination Quillon will export all customer data within 15 business
days.

## Data protection

Quillon processes visitor data only on the customer's instructions and
stores it in EU data centres.
