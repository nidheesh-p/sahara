# Thursday reading circle — October

Book: *The Salt Cartographer* by Ines Marrow
Host: Wren Ashby. Present: six members.

## Discussion

- Most of us loved the structure: each chapter is one of the mapmaker's
  charts of the drowned coastline, annotated in her own hand.
- Debate about whether the brother, Tobiah, is a reliable narrator in the
  final third. Half the group thinks the lighthouse fire never happened.
- The salt-flat imagery felt repetitive to some readers by the midpoint.
- Favourite line: "Every map is a promise the sea has not agreed to."

## Ratings

Average 4.1 out of 5.
