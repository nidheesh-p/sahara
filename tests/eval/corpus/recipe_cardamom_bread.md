# Grandma Ilse's cardamom braid

A soft, lightly sweet yeasted loaf flavoured with freshly ground cardamom.
Makes one large braid.

## Ingredients

- 500 g strong white flour
- 7 g instant yeast
- 70 g caster sugar
- 2 tsp cardamom seeds, crushed
- 250 ml warm whole milk
- 80 g soft butter
- 1 egg, plus 1 yolk for glazing
- Pearl sugar for the top

## Method

1. Mix flour, yeast, sugar and cardamom. Add milk, egg and butter and knead
   for ten minutes until smooth and elastic.
2. Cover and let rise for about an hour, until doubled.
3. Divide into three ropes and plait them into a braid.
4. Proof for 30 minutes, brush with yolk and scatter pearl sugar.
5. Bake at 190 °C for 25 minutes until deep golden.

Best eaten the same day, still slightly warm.
