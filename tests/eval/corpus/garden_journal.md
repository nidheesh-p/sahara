# Allotment journal — summer

## Early June

Planted out eight tomato seedlings (two each of Sungold, Black Krim,
San Marzano and a yellow pear variety) along the south fence. Added
canes and soft ties. Courgettes and runner beans going in next weekend.

## Mid June

Found clusters of green aphids under the tomato leaves and on the bean
tips. Sprayed with diluted soap solution and pinched off the worst
shoots. Planted marigolds and nasturtiums between rows to draw
ladybirds in.

## July

Watering schedule: deep soak every second evening, more often in
heatwaves. Feeding the tomatoes weekly with comfrey liquid once the first
trusses set. First Sungold picked on 14 July.

## Notes for next year

Start seeds two weeks earlier and give the beans a taller frame.
